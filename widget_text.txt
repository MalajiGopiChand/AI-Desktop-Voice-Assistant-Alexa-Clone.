Redmi is a sub-brand of Xiaomi, a Chinese electronics company. Redmi is known for producing affordable smartphones with high-end features.

Here are some key features of Redmi smartphones:

1. **Affordable prices**: Redmi smartphones are generally priced lower than other flagship brands.
2. **High-end features**: Redmi smartphones often come with advanced features like high-resolution cameras, fast charging, and large displays.
3. **Xiaomi ecosystem**: Redmi smartphones are part of the Xiaomi ecosystem, which means they can be easily integrated with other Xiaomi devices and services.

Some popular Redmi smartphone models include:

1. **Redmi Note series**: Known for their large displays and long battery life.
2. **Redmi 9 series**: Offers a balance of performance and affordability.
3. **Redmi K series**: Focuses on gaming performance and high-end features.
